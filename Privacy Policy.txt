# Hermes – Privacy Policy

**Bot Name:** Hermes  
**Discord Username:** Hermess

**Last Updated:** November 18, 2026

## 1. Introduction
Hermes is a private Discord bot connected to a locally hosted AI system. It is intended exclusively for the bot owner and one whitelisted user.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed when interacting with Hermes.

## 2. Information Collected
Hermes may process the following information:
- Discord usernames and user IDs for whitelist verification.
- Messages sent to the bot.
- Commands and interactions with the bot.
- Information provided by users during conversations.
- Technical logs generated during bot operation.

Hermes does not intentionally collect information unrelated to its functionality.

## 3. How Information Is Used
Information processed by Hermes is used exclusively to:
- Verify whether a user is authorized to access the bot.
- Process messages and generate AI responses.
- Maintain and troubleshoot the bot.
- Improve the functionality and reliability of the system.

## 4. Data Storage
Hermes uses a locally hosted AI environment. Depending on its configuration, conversation history, logs, or other information may be stored locally on the owner's machine.

Local hosting does not necessarily mean all data stays local. Discord and any external AI services or APIs used by Hermes may independently process information.

## 5. Data Sharing
Hermes does not intentionally sell or publicly distribute user conversations or personal information.

Information may be transmitted to third-party services when required for Discord connectivity, AI processing, or other enabled integrations.

Those services may process data under their own privacy policies.

## 6. Data Retention & Deletion
Conversation history, logs, and other stored information may be retained for as long as necessary for the bot's operation.

Users can contact the bot owner to request the deletion of information stored locally by Hermes.

Some information retained by Discord or third-party services may need to be deleted through those services separately.

## 7. Security
Reasonable measures are taken to restrict access to Hermes and its locally stored data.

However, no system can guarantee complete security, and users should avoid sharing passwords, financial information, or other highly sensitive information.

## 8. Policy Changes
This Privacy Policy may be updated at any time. Changes will apply once the updated policy is published.

## 9. Contact
For privacy-related questions or data deletion requests, contact the owner of Hermes directly through Discord.

---

**Hermes is a private personal AI project. Your information is processed only to support its functionality, subject to the local configuration and third-party services involved.**
